Side effects of Interface elements on boundary

Applying interface elements on the boundary has the following side effects:

  • If the top surface is a free surface (sea bottom or ground surfaces), then the Top K-values should be set at very low values (order 1E-6 MPa/m) in order not to restrain deformation. The applied stress tensor pointset to the top surface will dominate.
  • One can not set other boundary conditions, like described for meshes without boundary elements at the sides. 
  • All initial stresses come from the applied 3D stress tensor pointset. The pointset acts to preload the elements (so that they do not need to deform to reach these stresses), but also acts on the boundaries of the model. These stresses have to be in line with gravity build up and the expected horizontal stresses. The iterative initial stress routine, applying the “material properties” of initial horizontal/vertical stress ratios “Kh” and “KH” will be fully ignored.
  • The seawater loading is fully ignored. All total stress effects of seawater need to be included in the 3D stress pointset near the top surface
