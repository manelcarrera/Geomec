Dynamically zooming, rotating and moving

The following dynamic view changes of the drawing area are available:

In Default and Top View:

Rotate

Left-click and drag in the drawing area to rotate dynamically.

Move

Left-click and drag in the drawing area while pressing the Ctrl button to move side/upwards dynamically.

Zoom in/out

Left-click and drag in the drawing area while pressing the Shift button to zoom dynamically. Drag upward to zoom in and downward to zoom out.

 

In Birdeye View (Gocad control system):

Rotate

Left-click and drag in the drawing area to rotate dynamically.

Move

Middle-click and drag in the drawing area to move side/upwards dynamically (shift view).

Zoom in/out

Right-click and drag in the drawing area to zoom dynamically. Drag upward to zoom in and downward to zoom out.
