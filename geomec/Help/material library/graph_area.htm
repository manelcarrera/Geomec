The graphs area of the Material Parameters dialogHIDD_PARAMETERSDLG

 

The graphs area can contain graphs of

·         The axial strain as a function of the effective axial stress.

·         The radial strain as a function of the effective axial stress.

or

·         P-Q plots

dependent on the selection in the Graph area on the Experiment TabHIDD_TABEXPERIMENT.

 

Each graph has a legend.

 

A context menu is available when the right mouse button is clicked on one of the graphs. The following options are supplied:

·         Copy

·         Save As

·         Print
