Insert Menu

The Insert menu contains the following items.

Cross section > Graphically

To insert a cross section graphically.

Cross section > By value

To insert a cross section by value, directly in the cross section attributes dialog.

Deviated wellpath

To insert a deviated wellpath.

Vertical wellpath > Graphically

To insert a wellpath graphically.

Vertical wellpath > By value

To insert a cross section by value, directly in the wellpath attributes dialog.
