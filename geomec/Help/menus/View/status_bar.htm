Status Bar

The status bar is the horizontal grey bar at the bottom of the main window and displays general information like the unit system and analysis type.

Selecting status bar in the View Menu toggles the status bar on and off.

Clicking on SI or FIELD in the status bar switches between the two unit types.

When LIN is displayed in the status bar linear results are available, when NONLIN is displayed in the status bar nonlinear results are available.  If CALC is displayed in the status bar no results are available.
