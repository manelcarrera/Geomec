Shape Factor

Shape factor is a quality Mesh result of the mesh results branch in the model tree.  Shape factor is based on an algorithm to check mesh quality according to the theory provided in Roddeman & Jansen.  "An a priori geometry check for a single isoparametric element.  Computers & Structures 47, 1 (1993).

Other quality mesh results are:

Max edge length described by the value of the longest edge length of an element.

Average edge length described by the average length of the edges of an element.

Area described by the total area of all the surfaces of an element.

Volume described by the volume of an element.

Min/Max edge ratio described by the ratio of shortest to longest edge length of an element.

Maximum angle described by the maximum angle between two edges of an element.

Minimum angle described by the minimum angle between two edges of an element.
