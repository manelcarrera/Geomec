Area

Area is a quality Mesh result in the mesh results branch in the model tree. Area represents the total area of all the surfaces of an element.

Other quality mesh results are:

Max edge length described by the value of the longest edge length (distance between two nodes) of an element.

Average edge length described by the average length of the edges of an element.

Volume described by the volume of an element.

Min/Max edge ratio described by the ratio of shortest to longest edge length of an element. A very low value will give inaccurate results or even unstable calculations.

Maximum angle described by the maximum angle between two edges of an element. Too large (close to 180 degrees) or too small (less than 5 degrees) give bad element performance

Minimum angle described by the minimum angle between two edges of an element.

Shape Factor described by an algorithm to check mesh quality.  For background theory refer to:  Roddeman & Jansen, "An a priori geometry check for a single isoparametric element.  Computers & Structures 47, 1 (1993).
