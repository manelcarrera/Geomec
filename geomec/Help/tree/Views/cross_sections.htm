Cross section view

When the cross section view is selected, only cross sections can be selected in the model tree, and only one at a time. The cross section view shows the cross section as a vertical plane on which (in results mode) results can be mapped or (in geology mode) using the colors of the formations being crossed.

If the right mouse button is clicked in the view area, a context menu appears with the option to flip the view. This will allow you to look at the same cross section from the other side.
