Weight of seawater

Weight of seawater is a leaf in the Model tree and is used to define the pressure loading on the top of the model (the sea floor) due to the weight of seawater above.

The weight of seawater must be entered manually as a water level depth coordinate and a water column stress gradient.

Context sensitive menu

Right-clicking on Weight of seawater in the model tree displays the following context sensitive menu:

Copy

To copy the weight of seawater (not supported yet).

Attributes

To view or change the weight of seawater attributes.
