Change position cross section

The position of a previously defined cross section can be changed graphically. Right-click on the desired cross section in the model tree and select change position. Left-click on one of the two ends of the cross section line (a green square) and drag it to a new position. Left-click somewhere else in the view to assert the new position.

Use attributes to view the cross section attributes.

Use insert cross section to create a new cross section.

Use delete to delete previously created cross section.
