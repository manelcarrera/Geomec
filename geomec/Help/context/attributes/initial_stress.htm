Initial stress attributes

The initial stress attributes dialog is displayed by right-clicking on the Initial stress branch in the model tree and selecting Attributes.

The initial stress situation in the model is calculated from the densities of the materials and the gravity acceleration constant. This is an iterative process in which GEOMEC tries to find an equilibrium. If the analysis is to include the effects of overlying water (i.e. is an off-shore field) then the initial stress attribute dialog allows the specification of a water level depth coordinate and a water column stress gradient. For further information on the initial stress procedure see background.
