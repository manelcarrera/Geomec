Reset limits

Reset limits is used to reset (or change) the limits of the 2D-line graph. The graph limits dialog appears. Default the upper and lower limits of the Y-axis (TVD or AHD) are given. The values can be altered so that clicking Ok will "reset" to those values.

Zooming in on a section of the graph can also be done by clicking in the graph and dragging up or down. After releasing of the mouse button the graph is displayed in between the new limits.

The reset limits option can be executed by right-clicking on the 2D-Line graph and selecting Reset limits.
