Fault frictional behavior

The interface of a fault is governed by a frictional behavior. This behavior can be modeled with the Coulomb friction model, which has a close resemblance with the Mohr-Coulomb plasticity model for continuum elements. In GEOMEC the elastic fault stiffness is chosen very stiff such that elastic fault slip can be neglected. For sticking faults the normal and shear stiffnesses are 1000 GPa/m. For full slip fault the shear stiffness is reduced to 1 MPa/m. The elastic defaults for Mohr-Coulomb slip are 500 and 50 GPa/m for the normal and shear stiffness.
