Nonlinear Plasticity material models

The fundamental difference between elastic and plastic strain is that elastic strain is directly associated with (or linked to) a change in stress. Within the plastic strain there is still the (rather irrelevant) distinction between plastic strains that depend on loading only (plastic strain) and strain which depends on loads and applied loading time (creep strain). The elastic strain is not a standard output of Geomec but is the difference between the total strain minus the plastic strain minus the creep strain.

 

The following plasticity / creep models can be used in GEOMEC:

Mohr-Coulomb plasticity

Mohr-Coulomb with cohesion or friction hardening

Cam-clay

Cam-clay creep

Modified Mohr Coulomb

Salt creep model

 

Fault frictional behavior describes the nonlinear behavior of the faults
